About Me

I am Winkelmann17, and I am a software dev / cyber professional. In 2024 I graduated with my Bachelor's in Cyber Engineering and I am about to complete my Master's in Cybersecurity Science. Shortly after graduating with my Bachelor's I became a full-time software engineer.
